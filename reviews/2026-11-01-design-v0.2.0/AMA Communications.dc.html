01 · The problem

Business owners have tools and information, but often need help deciding where to act.

Too much informationDocuments and conversations do not automatically become a shared understanding.

Reasoning

Next action
A responsible owner, a next action and a measure of usefulness.
Review
A human reviewer and a date or event that requires reassessment.
