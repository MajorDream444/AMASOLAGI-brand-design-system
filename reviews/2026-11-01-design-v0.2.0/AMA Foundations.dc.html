Recommendation · resolves the CSS / brand book conflict

Follow the brand book: Source Serif 4 for doc-title and doc-h1, IBM Plex Sans for doc-h2 and body, JetBrains Mono for labels, IDs and dates. Tables move to IBM Plex Sans 13px rather than mono, because proposals carry long text in cells and mono widens them. The serif gives documents a distinct, considered voice next to the Inter-led website, while mono labels keep the two related. Fallbacks: Georgia, system-ui, ui-monospace. Until approved, documents may render entirely in fallbacks without breaking layout.
