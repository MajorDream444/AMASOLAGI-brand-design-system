After work begins, cancellation is assessed against completed work and documented nonrecoverable costs; any unearned balance is refunded. Proposal · AMA-P-0007 · Draft v0.3

A clearer client intake for Halden Physiotherapy

Prepared for R. Mensah, Practice Lead · Prepared by AMA SOL AGI · 29 Oct 2026

Fictional client·Badge on dark uses in_review cut-out·Valid until [confirm]

01 · Executive overview

New patients currently reach Halden by phone, email and a web form, and the front desk re-enters the same details three times. We propose a single intake workflow, reviewed by a named person at Halden before anything reaches a clinician.

Problem

Duplicate entry, missed referral details, and no record of who checked each intake.

Intended outcome

One intake path with a human review step and a log of each decision. Not a guarantee of time saved.

Scope

New-patient intake only. Existing patient records, billing and clinical notes are out of scope.

02 · Deliverables and exclusions

Included

  • Source and requirements map
  • Intake question set, reviewed with you
  • Workflow prototype with human review step
  • Handover notes and a 30-minute walkthrough

Not included

  • Hosting or third-party software licences
  • Changes to clinical systems
  • Ongoing support after handover [optional add-on]
