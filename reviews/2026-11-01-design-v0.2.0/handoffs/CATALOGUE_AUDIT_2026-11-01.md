Reviewed: `AMA Applications.dc.html` (catalogue data after the STD fix, 1 Nov 2026), `AMA Front Door.dc.html`, `AMA Components.dc.html`, `AMA Communications.dc.html`. Sources: founder summaries pasted 1 Nov 2026; AMA Strategy and Offers PDF (30 Oct 2026).
Catalogue unchanged in scope: 12 one-time, 6 monthly, 2 consulting, 1 implementation route. Prices and availability stages unchanged. Purchase gated.

## Fixed in this pass
1. **Unsupported promise (Front Door).** The recommendation for Make It Make Sense Kit said it delivers "a plan you can run yourself". The kit is a video, FAQ and one-page explanation. Copy now matches the deliverables, inputs and 3-day delivery.
2. **Missing support on monthly cards.** The six monthly cards showed no revision terms. Each now shows its per-request revision rule and, where the source states one, what is not included (live consulting, news monitoring, publishing, continuous monitoring).
3. **Knowledge Continuity.** The monthly change summary arriving with the 2nd refresh was missing from the card; added to Support.
4. **Legal name.** Every visible "AMA Solutions Corporation" now reads "(legal name to verify)". Not used in live billing or finalised policy.

## Open: need founder decision
1. **SKUs.** The catalogue carries no SKUs or product IDs. None appear in the strategy PDF pages reviewed. Send the SKU list to attach them, or confirm none exist yet.
2. **Legacy offer names elsewhere.** Components (product card "Expertise inventory", bundle "Practitioner system kit", IDs AMA-R-01–03) and Communications (square tile "Expertise inventory · AMA-R-01") use example offers from v0.2.0 that are not in the catalogue. Recommend relabelling them with real catalogue offers (e.g. Walk In Ready, Make It Make Sense Kit).
3. **Bundles.** The strategy PDF holds bundles. Components still shows a bundle card as a pattern; confirm it stays as a pattern only.
4. **Refund wording conflict.** Monthly terms promise "missed accepted work is completed or proportionally refunded". One-time offers have no stated refund rule, and the draft Terms (PR #7) say refunds are set per product. Needs a one-time refund line before publishing.
5. **Turnaround start.** Summaries say turnaround begins "after payment and acceptance of complete inputs". The proposal and front door say kickoff and booking follow payment. Consistent, but "acceptance of complete inputs" has no UI state yet (no "inputs accepted" step in onboarding or workspace).
6. **Video claims.** Four offers include video. No video production capacity is evidenced in the package; confirm capacity before availability moves from "to confirm".
7. **Source units over limit.** The terms say larger sources count as several units, but no UI shows a running unit count or what happens when a client exceeds it.
