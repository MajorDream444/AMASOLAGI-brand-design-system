AMA Solutions is a human-led, AI-enabled strategic services corporation. The identity is elegant, intelligent, warm, precise, modern, quietly ambitious and accountable: near-black and charcoal, warm metallic gold, generous negative space, and the founder-approved AMA badge used as a seal, never as wallpaper.

Two layers live in this system. **Website** tokens (`bg`, `gold`, `text-secondary`…) are exact values from the live amasolagi.com code. **Brand** tokens (`brand-*`, `status-*`) and the "Proposed brand type" styles come from the corporate brand handoff and are PROVISIONAL until colors are sampled from the approved badge and fonts are approved. Use website tokens for the website; use brand tokens for documents, decks and print, and label anything built on them DRAFT.

## Content fundamentals

- **Company line:** "AMA Solutions is a human-led, AI-enabled strategic services corporation."
- **Value:** "We help organizations make complex opportunities actionable through strategy, advisory, and well-scoped AI-enabled work."
- **Operating principle:** "Digital workers extend the reach of capable people; accountable humans remain responsible for decisions and delivery."
- **Voice:** direct, strategic, composed, warm. Explain complex systems in plain language. Say who is responsible, what happens next and what is still in development. Confident without hype.
- **Name:** public-facing "AMA Solutions". The legal entity name only on contracts, invoices, tax, banking and legal notices, and only once its exact suffix is confirmed.
- **Preferred words:** digital workers, human-led, AI-enabled, strategic advisory, clear accountability, designed capacity, in development, available now (only when true). Define "AGInt" whenever it appears in public copy.
- **Never:** promise autonomous outcomes or guaranteed results; imply the 144-worker system is deployed (it is a founding symbol and design framework); invent clients, testimonials, certifications, prices, staffing, legal clauses, integrations or partner endorsements. Label every service as planned, prototyped, in development or verified live.
- **Ecosystem:** AMA stays independent and client-facing. HAMAL coordinates AMA, Hanzo and Lux; never present Hanzo or Lux as AMA departments or imply a partner commitment without approval. Founder doctrine and biography go to majordreamwilliams.com; AI education to majoraimindset.com.
- **Stage words matter:** keep an inquiry, a proposal, an accepted contract, a paid order and completed delivery distinct in copy and documents.
- **Casing:** sentence case for headings; all caps only for short labels, buttons and formal document metadata. No emoji.

## Identity

- The founder-approved badge (gold circular medallion, sculptural AMA monogram with small evenly spaced head/node forms) is the master mark; the standalone monogram is the same identity without the outer ring. The heads represent the collective digital workforce: keep their rhythm even and never let them read as a crowd, faces or confetti, or as a literal headcount.
- Use the dimensional badge only on covers and hero moments; use the flat master for forms, small screens, embossing and one-color work.
- Never redraw, add a ring, simplify the nodes or swap in a generic AI symbol. If no vector exists, a faithful vectorization goes to the founder for review.
- Files: `ama-badge.png` (master), `ama-monogram.png` and `ama-lockup-horizontal.png` in the Logos group. They are flattened rasters on light grounds with no vector yet: use them for on-screen hero and cover moments on light or cream grounds, and get a founder-approved vectorization before favicons, one-color, print or pin production.
- The horizontal lockup sets "AMA" in near-black over "SOLUTIONS" in gold capitals and works on light grounds only.
- The website still uses its interim gold-square-and-triangle mark; do not use that mark on new brand documents.

## Color

- **Website:** dark only. Ground `bg`; cards and inputs on `surface` with a 1px `border`; nest `surface-2`, then `surface-3`. Headlines in `text`, paragraphs and links in `text-secondary`. `text-dim` and `gold-dim` (4.1:1 on `bg`) are for small labels, borders and numerals only. `gold` is the single accent (primary button, eyebrows, focus ring, one highlighted word per headline); text on `gold` is `bg` ink. `gold-light` is hover/active; `gold-glow` and `gold-glow-sm` are washes, never text.
- **Brand (provisional):** dark field `brand-obsidian` with `brand-charcoal` panels; light field and paper `brand-warm-white` with `brand-obsidian` text. `brand-champagne-gold` is for dark grounds; on paper it is decorative rules only (1.9:1). Gold on light grounds is `brand-deep-gold` at 24px+ or as rules and icons.
- `brand-slate` (4.2:1 on paper) misses AA for small text; keep it to 18px+ until a darker value is approved.
- **Status** (`status-green`, `status-amber`, `status-red`) is functional only and never competes with the identity. Every status carries a word and an icon: green and red are almost the same lightness.
- Gold is a material, not a fill for every surface. No chrome, lens flares, glowing circuitry, particle effects or blue-purple gradients.
- Stripe's dashboard charcoal #1E1E1E and blue #44B1FF are not brand colors; leave them until a Stripe application is reviewed.

## Typography

- **Website:** Inter (`display`) at 500 with tight tracking for headlines (`display-xl` hero only, `display-lg`, `display-md`, `display-sm`, fluid in code; token values are maximums). JetBrains Mono (`mono`) for everything else: `body`, `body-mono`, `body-sm`, `button`, `nav-link`, `label-mono`.
- **Brand documents (proposed, pending approval):** Source Serif 4 (`serif`) for `doc-title` and `doc-h1`; IBM Plex Sans (`sans`) for `doc-h2`, `doc-body`, `doc-small`; JetBrains Mono for `doc-label` (IDs, dates, status, folios) and `doc-table`. All three are SIL Open Font License on Google Fonts; IBM Plex Sans has an Arabic companion for Gulf and North African work. Package links and licenses, never unlicensed files.
- Contracts and invoices put legibility ahead of styling: `doc-body` at 10.5pt in print, nothing below 8pt.

## Spacing, radii and layout

- Website content sits in a 1200px `container` with a `spacing-card` gutter; every section gets `spacing-section` top and bottom; cards pad `spacing-card`; grid gaps `spacing-gap`; inline gaps `spacing-base`.
- Cards and panels use `radius-card`; buttons, badges and nav pills `radius-pill`; utility chips `radius-sm`.
- Documents use generous margins, strong alignment and predictable grids, with thin gold rules, structured columns, modular panels, quiet section numbering and folios.

## Graphic language and imagery

- Supporting geometry, used sparingly and always secondary: circular badge geometry, fine concentric rules, 12-part or 12×12 grid references, node motifs. Never present decorative dots as a map of the deployed workforce.
- The website hero's `grid-overlay` (80px hairline grid in `border`) is the digital expression of the same idea.
- Diagrams explain a real process, responsibility or service, never "AI network" decoration.
- Photography: real people, real work, architecture, materials, understated editorial light. Show human judgment alongside software. No robot stock, humanoid heads, glowing brains or random code. Generated images are mood exploration only. The Imagery group's campus render is labelled concept.

## Borders, shadows and states

- Borders define surfaces at rest; shadows appear only on hover (`shadow-card-hover`, `shadow-card-gold-hover` with a 2px lift, `shadow-btn-primary-hover`).
- Focus is a solid 2px `gold` outline with a 3px offset (8.3:1 on `bg`).
- Active nav item: `gold-light` text on `gold-glow-sm` with a 20% gold border.

## Motion

- Deliberate and slow: a turn of light across the medallion, a reveal of the nodes, a clean emblem-to-diagram transition. No flashing or particle swarms.
- Website easing `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; 150ms controls, 250ms cards, 700ms entrance reveals (`fadeUp` 24px, `fadeIn`, `maskReveal`).
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: animation and transition durations collapse.

## Iconography

- The brand glyph is the badge. UI icons today are small inline SVGs in `currentColor` (hamburger, close); a consistent-stroke icon family is still to be designed.

## Departments

- One parent identity. Executive Office, Strategy & Advisory, Delivery & Client Success, AI & Digital Workforce, Product & Engineering, Finance & Operations, Legal & Risk, People & Talent, Marketing & Communications and Partnerships are named with type and labels (`doc-label`), never separate logos. These are placeholders: use owner-approved role and title fields, and leave unstaffed functions off public materials.

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA for all digital text and controls; check every gold pairing, since gold is not legible on every ground.
- Status by label and icon plus color. Tagged PDFs, alt text, keyboard-friendly forms, editable text (never flattened forms).
- Test the mark at favicon, avatar, letterhead, deck, invoice and pin scale.

## Components

Button, Badge, Card, Wordmark, Nav and NewsletterSignup are the website's reusable pieces, shown as static previews styled by `components/bundle.css` (copied from the site's `globals.css`). Governance, file naming and owner inputs: see the Governance section.

## Not synced

- Motion tokens (`--ease-*`, `--duration-*`) have no token family; they are documented under Motion.
- No font files are in the repository; Inter and JetBrains Mono are Google-hosted.
- The site is a Next.js app, not a component library: static previews only; `HeroCanvas`, `SchemaOrg` and `ContactForm` left out.
- Next.js boilerplate SVGs in `public/` skipped.
