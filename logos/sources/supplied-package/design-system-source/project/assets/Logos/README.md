# Logos

The founder-approved AMA identity, as supplied by the founder. Use these files exactly as they are; never redraw, recolor, add a ring or simplify the node pattern.

- `ama-badge.png` — **the master mark.** The full badge: a dimensional gold medallion with a fine double rim around a black field, holding the sculptural AMA monogram with gold node forms set into its three triangular openings. 1024 × 1024. Use on covers, hero moments, social avatars and anywhere the mark is the subject.
- `ama-monogram.png` — the standalone monogram: the same three interlocking gold triangles and nodes without the medallion. 788 × 580. Use where a circle would be cramped or the badge is already present on the page.
- `ama-lockup-horizontal.png` — the horizontal lockup: badge left, "AMA" in near-black over "SOLUTIONS" in gold capitals, right. Light grounds only, since the black "AMA" disappears on dark. 1024 × 1024 canvas with wide empty margins: crop to the artwork, don't scale the canvas.

All three are flattened RGB rasters with no transparency: the badge and monogram sit on a warm cream ground, the lockup on white. Place them on matching light grounds, or on `brand-obsidian` only once a transparent cut-out exists. They are not production masters: there is no vector yet, so for favicons, small sizes, one-color, embossing, print and the lapel pin a faithful vectorization must be made and approved by the founder first. Do not rasterize these up beyond their native size.

Keep the dimensional badge for hero use. At 32px and below the nodes merge into texture, which is why a flat vector master is required for small sizes.

Interim, website only (copied verbatim from the inline SVG in the site's Nav and Footer, which predates the badge):

- `ama-mark.svg` — gold (`gold`, #C9A227) 28px square with 6px corners holding a near-black (`bg`, #080808) upward triangle.
- `ama-glyph.svg` — the 14px triangle alone, ink `bg` (#080808), only on a `gold` field.

Do not use the interim mark on new brand documents, decks or print. It stays only until the website switches to the badge.
