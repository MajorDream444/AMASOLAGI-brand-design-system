# Governance

## Status labels

Every brand file carries one status: **DRAFT**, **REVIEW**, **APPROVED** or **DEPRECATED**. Anything built on provisional `brand-*` tokens or the proposed type is DRAFT. Never overwrite an APPROVED master; record a new version instead, with owner and approver fields.

## File naming

`AMA_[Department]_[Asset]_[Format]_[Locale]_v###_[Status]`

Example: `AMA_Finance_Invoice_USLetter_EN_v001_DRAFT.docx`

## Placeholders and sensitive fields

- Mark every unconfirmed value as a placeholder: legal entity name, address, phone, email, tax IDs, banking and payment instructions, titles, prices.
- Legal documents (NDA, MSA, consulting, subcontractor agreements) are design shells only; counsel supplies and reviews every clause. Designers and AI tools never draft legal terms.
- Keep real financial and legal details out of public templates until the owner authorizes them. QR codes point only to an approved public destination.

## Approval gates

1. **Reference:** the exact founder-approved badge and lockup are the source; no redesign.
2. **Identity standards:** founder approves sampled colors, final fonts, clear space, minimum size and the flat master.
3. **Templates:** founder and department owners approve the document inventory and editable fields.
4. **Legal and finance:** counsel approves contract language; finance confirms entity, tax, invoice and payment fields.
5. **Digital:** web owner checks contrast, responsive crops, metadata and fit with the current site.
6. **Production proof:** print proof and physical pin sample approved before manufacturing.
7. **Release:** publish the library with approved files, version numbers and a short usage guide.

## Owner inputs still needed

- Exact legal entity name and approved public short name.
- The approved badge and monogram masters (vector if available), with light and dark lockups.
- Final color values sampled from the badge artwork (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone/metallic only after proofing).
- Approval of the proposed fonts (Source Serif 4, IBM Plex Sans, JetBrains Mono) or alternatives.
- Postal address, phone, email, website, tax details and invoice/payment instructions.
- Approved executive titles and department names; unstaffed roles stay off public materials.
- Counsel's contract templates and jurisdictional language.
- The confirmed advisory and product catalog and payment links before any pricing appears.
- Approved social accounts, URLs, biography and boilerplate.
- Pin vendor, method, dimensions and budget.

## Checklist for staff, contractors and AI tools

- Is the badge the approved master, unaltered?
- Does every claim describe something available now, or is it labelled planned, prototyped or in development?
- Are all unconfirmed fields marked as placeholders?
- Does every text pairing pass WCAG AA, and does every status have a word and an icon?
- Is the file named and labelled with a status and version?
