# AMA strategic design update - 31 October 2026

Status: in_review. Source: Major's October 31 conversation with Pat, supplied transcript and Major's subsequent instruction to update this repository. This records meeting inputs and design requirements; it does not approve prices, a rebrand, infrastructure migration or live capabilities.

## Offer and customer experience
Major diagnoses the business problem, curates the appropriate Art Mob team, and provides accountable implementation and support. Clients buy useful work getting done. Start with practitioners and businesses with repeated operational work; wellness practitioners are an initial focus, not the only audience.

Journey: explain the problem -> understand recommendation -> choose resource or consultation -> agree scope -> implementation -> ongoing support.

Design a "Tell us what you need help with" front door: proposed text conversation, optional voice, clear AI identification, editable needs summary and explained recommendation. Retain direct browsing and human contact. Route to practical resources, consultation or implementation. Voice and routing are proposals until implemented.

## Art Mob team component
Show role, business task, inputs, outputs, human approval boundaries, operating schedule, current status and support owner. Clients may need only a few roles. The 12 mobs/144-agent framework is not proof of deployment. HAMAL coordinates Hanzo, AMA and Lux. Preserve each mob's personality without obscuring practical responsibilities.

## Pricing and usage
Separate initial fees, recurring management/support, included allowances and extra usage. Show caps, alerts, explicit recharge consent, receipts, exhaustion/pause states and recovery. Never fabricate consumption, silently increase charges or imply continuous agent execution when tasks run on a schedule.
The meeting mentions $350 for a one-hour consultation: confirm against the current catalogue before publishing. Subscription and recharge figures discussed are illustrative, not approved prices. Bulk-credit margins and model routing economics need actual supplier costs and terms; do not assume a discount.
Customer funds, AMA service payments, usage charges and partner compensation require separately defined flows. Advanced payout routing and autonomous spending are outside this design task.
