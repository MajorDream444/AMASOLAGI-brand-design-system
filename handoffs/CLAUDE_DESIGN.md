# AMA Solutions Design System — opening brief

Build a reusable AMA Solutions Design System from the supplied approved AMA materials. Begin by auditing source assets and prior brand decisions. Preserve AMA's identity and separate established rules from proposals and missing specifications.

AMA Solutions helps existing practitioners and businesses turn expertise and repeated work into practical AI-powered systems. Its applications include commerce, consulting, automation, client delivery, and productized resources. Website: amasolagi.com. Legal entity: AMA Solutions Inc.

Use approved logo assets and documented color/font values. Do not borrow MAIM Academy's crest, palette, or learning treatments without an explicit AMA decision supporting that use.

Define logo usage; typography; colors and accessible combinations; spacing and grids; buttons, forms, navigation, cards and status indicators; product and bundle cards; consulting/booking flows; proposal and onboarding layouts; client workspace components; presentation and social templates.

Demonstrate one product catalogue section, one consulting proposal page, and one client workspace overview. Use fictional client data. Keep changing prices, dates, product availability, and campaign copy editable. New layouts and specifications remain proposals until reviewed.

Return editable outputs, export specifications, unresolved decisions, and implementation instructions. Approved changes return to GitHub through a pull request. Do not assume Claude Design automatically synchronizes to the repository.
